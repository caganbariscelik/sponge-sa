XYLEM INNOVATION CHALLENGE

SPONGE-SA

Let cities soak up rain like a sponge, not flood.

The problem

Impermeable surfaces like concrete and asphalt do not absorb rainwater. Water pools quickly on the surface and raises the risk of urban flooding.

The solution

A sponge structure of grass, soil, gravel and drainage layers slows the water, holds it and releases it in a controlled way. We measure its effect on a scaled model with two experiments.

Model A

💧💧💧
Impermeable sloped ground. Water pools and runs off the surface.

Model B

💧💧💧
Grass, soil, gravel and drainage. Water is held back and slowed.

How we measure

  1. Same rainfall
    We pour the same amount of water onto both models in the same time.
  2. Measure
    We record surface outflow, drainage and flow start time over 3 repeats.
  3. Compare
    The sponge effect and flood risk are calculated automatically on the results screen.
